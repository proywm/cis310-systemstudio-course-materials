## fflate

The extension bundles the `fflate` npm package to read the checksum-verified ZIP archive. SystemStudio validates every archive path and applies file-count and uncompressed-size limits before writing entries. The complete MIT license is included at `third_party/fflate-LICENSE.txt`.
