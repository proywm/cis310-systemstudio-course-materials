## fflate

The extension bundles the `fflate` npm package to read the checksum-verified ZIP archive. SystemStudio validates every archive path and applies file-count and uncompressed-size limits before writing entries. The complete MIT license is included at `third_party/fflate-LICENSE.txt`.

The preview image in `media/half-adder-preview.png` was generated by Digital v0.31 from its upstream `examples/combinatorial/HalfAdder.dig` example and is included for documentation and attribution.

## Portable Assembly Lab container

The VSIX includes a Dockerfile and runner script, not a prebuilt Linux image or the toolchain binaries. After explicit confirmation, Docker downloads the digest-pinned Debian Bookworm Slim base and Debian packages for NASM, GNU binutils, Make, and GDB. Their respective licenses and package copyright files are included in the resulting local image under `/usr/share/doc`.
