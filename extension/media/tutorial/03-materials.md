# Embedded materials, authoritative Canvas

SystemStudio uses one preparation sequence for every lecture: **Read → Watch → Try 3 questions**. The mapped reading comes from focused sections of David Tarnoff's required open textbook, and the companion comes from the author's official YouTube channel or ETSU OER series. Those authoritative sources open externally; the extension does not copy the book or use Google Drive.

SystemStudio includes these resources inside the extension:

- 13 offline, integrity-checked lecture PDFs;
- Homework 1, Homework 2, and Homework 3 references; and
- three processor-project references.

The Learning Center maps the open-book chapters and author videos to all 13 presentation resources, then adds 43 short formative questions with at least three for every lecture resource. It supports immediate-feedback practice, end-of-session quiz review, confidence checks, saved questions, and local spaced review.

The presentations are packaged locally, so no external document-hosting account is needed. The Fall 2026 Canvas course controls deadlines, requirements, and submission.
