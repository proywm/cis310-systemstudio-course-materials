# Evaluation Instruments

These files form a deployable draft measurement package for SystemStudio AI. They are research materials, not classroom-ready forms.

## Required preparation

Do not collect student research data until all of the following are complete:

1. the appropriate U-M institutional determination is documented;
2. recruitment, consent, compensation, grade separation, and data handling match that determination;
3. an assessment specialist and course-content experts review the instruments;
4. the questionnaire is cognitively tested and revised;
5. assignment forms are piloted for content and difficulty equivalence;
6. graders are trained on anchor responses; and
7. the item IDs, scoring rules, primary outcome, and analysis plan are frozen.

Bracketed text is a field to be completed in the approved protocol. It must not be improvised during data collection.

## Administration principles

- Use a research-generated participant code; do not ask for name, email, UMID, uniqname, or student ID in a study instrument.
- Store any linkage key separately with access restricted to an authorized data steward.
- Make research participation voluntary and provide an equivalent nonresearch learning route.
- When practicable, keep the instructor and graders blind to participation until final grades are submitted.
- Do not use responses or study-assignment performance to determine course grades unless explicitly approved and participation does not change the grade opportunity.
- Put `Not applicable`, `Prefer not to answer`, or `I did not use this feature` where the distinction matters.
- Preserve item wording and response anchors between pre and post administrations.
- Never silently replace a missing response with the scale midpoint.

## Version control

Each exported instrument should record:

- protocol version;
- instrument version;
- course-pack version;
- extension version;
- AI model/configuration version, if applicable; and
- administration timestamp.

Changes after data collection begins require a version increment and a documented decision about whether responses remain comparable.
